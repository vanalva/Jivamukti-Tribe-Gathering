Coming Home in Rome

Jivamukti Tribe Gathering is a four-day yoga festival

celebrating life and expressing our love and devotion through collective yoga practice, chanting, meditation and satsang. For the first time ever, Rome welcomes the worldwide yoga community to join us at our annual gathering, featuring classes with top Jivamukti Yoga teachers from around the world.
