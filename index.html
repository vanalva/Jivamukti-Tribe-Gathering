JOIN THE TRIBE GATHERING

in Luxembourg

Nada Through Nadis

The roots of the lotus go deep into the Self. Through the roots, the channels we call nadis, we experience the eternal sound: nadam.

In a world focused on doing, on productivity and on taking sides, we lose the ability to inhabit the body. Once you are at peace, at home in yourself, you can care for others and support your community. In 2027 the Jivamukti Yoga Tribe Gathering brings the tribe to Luxembourg to practice this together.
