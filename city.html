ABOUT LUXEMBOURG

Your Luxembourg guide

You can find vegan options a little bit everywhere, but there aren't that many exclusively vegan restaurants in Luxembourg.

The first restaurants on the list are close to the Tribe location, but I would say the city centre is a better area overall, with more options and a nicer atmosphere.

Coque Luxembourg, 2 rue Léon Hengen, L-1745 Luxembourg

Getting around: see Mobiliteit.lu (opens in a new tab) for public transport and the Transport & practical information section at the end of the guide.

ABOUT THE TRIBE

Schueberfouer

During those days, the famous Schueberfouer (opens in a new tab) will be taking place near the city centre. It's a huge traditional funfair with lots of rides, games and food stalls (although vegan options are unfortunately very limited). It's a really popular event, so the city will be particularly lively and busy around that time!
